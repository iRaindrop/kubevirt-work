**Description**:
Add `search.suggest`, `search.highlight`, and `search.share` to the `theme.features` list in `mkdocs.yml`. This is a one-line-per-feature change that enables search suggestions, highlights search terms on the target page, and lets readers share a search URL.

**What you expected**:
Site search suggests completions as you type and highlights matching terms on the page you open.

**URL**:
`mkdocs.yml`

Site mechanics.
